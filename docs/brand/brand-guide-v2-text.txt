01 -- THE NAME

What RKADE means

RKADE is a constructed word where RK is capitalised intentionally. It stands for Raffay and
Kushan -- the two founders. The remaining letters form the word arcade.
The word arcade comes from the Latin arcus, meaning arch. Historically an arcade is a
series of arches -- a passageway that connects one place to another. Architecturally it is the
structure that holds things up while creating a path forward.
For RKADE, this is the core meaning: we are the passage businesses walk through to get
from where they are to where they want to be. We are the structure. We create the path.

Correct usage:
-- Written: RKade -- capital R, capital K, lowercase ade
-- Never: Rkade, rkade, RKADE (in body copy), R-Kade, R Kade
-- Domain: rkade.co
-- Email: contact@rkade.co / help@rkade.co
-- Tagline: AI Automation Consultancy · Dubai, UAE

Logo rules:
-- Minimum size: 120px wide for the horizontal lockup. 40px for the icon only.
-- Clear space: maintain clear space equal to the height of the K around the logo on all
sides.
-- Never stretch, rotate, recolour, or add effects to the logo.
-- Never place the logo on a busy background or any colour outside the brand palette.
-- Never recreate the logo in a different font or redraw the arch mark.
-- File formats: SVG for digital, PNG with transparent background for general use, PDF for
print. The original AI file is the master -- never edit anything else.

The RK treatment:
In the wordmark RKade, the letters RK are visually heavier than ade. This split is intentional
-- it represents the founders' initials embedded within a real word. Any reproduction of the
wordmark must preserve this visual distinction.

04 -- TYPOGRAPHY
Fonts

Two font families. One for display and headlines, one for body and UI. Never mixed beyond
their intended roles.

Primary -- Baile (Cormorant Garamond as digital substitute)
-- Role: Headlines, display text, the wordmark RKade, large numbers
-- Weight: Light (300) and Regular (400) for headlines. Semibold (600) for the wordmark
only.
-- Style: Serif. Elegant, editorial, premium. Never bold or heavy.
-- Digital substitute: Cormorant Garamond -- Google Fonts, free.

Secondary -- Montserrat
-- Role: Body copy, UI labels, captions, buttons, navigation
-- Weight: Light (300) for body. Medium (500) for labels and buttons.
-- Style: Clean geometric sans-serif. Pairs with the serif without competing.

Typography rules:
-- Headlines: Cormorant Garamond, light weight, large size, generous line height.
-- Body: Montserrat, light weight, 14-16px, 1.7-1.8 line height.
-- Never use bold body text except for emphasis on a specific word or phrase.
-- Italic in Cormorant Garamond is a feature -- use it for headline emphasis.
-- Letter spacing: 0.2em or higher for all-caps labels and eyebrows.

05 -- BRAND VOICE

How RKADE communicates

Direct, warm, and confident. Not academic. Not salesy. Not generic tech. RKADE speaks
like an expert who knows what they are doing and has no need to prove it.

Four principles:
-- Direct: Say what you mean. One sentence where others use three. No hedging.
-- Specific: Reference real things -- real numbers, real situations, real businesses.
-- Warm: Professional but human. Talking to a business owner, not writing a whitepaper.
-- Confident: Never apologise for what you do. State it plainly.

What to avoid:
-- AI buzzwords: leverage, synergy, ecosystem, holistic, cutting-edge, seamlessly, robust.
-- Em dashes in casual communication -- they read as formal and AI-generated.
-- Passive voice. Write actively.

Examples:
Wrong: 'We leverage cutting-edge AI technology to provide holistic solutions for your
business needs.'
Right: 'We build the system that books your next clients. You close.'
